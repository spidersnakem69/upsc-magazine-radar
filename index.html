How this was built, and what to trust

Source. Eight PDFs from your D drive. Five had a text layer. Yojana July, Kurukshetra July and Kurukshetra September were page images, so I read them with OCR. OCR text can drop or garble words, so counts for those three issues run low.

Counts. Keyword matches, so a scheme named differently is missed and a word used loosely is counted. Treat them as a guide to emphasis, not exact tallies.

Buckets, GS tags, priority. Assigned by me from the article titles and text. They are judgement calls. Tell me where you disagree and I will change them.

Facts. The facts in each watchlist item are what the magazine text says. The PDFs carry an upscpdf.com watermark and are not the official scans, so check key figures against PIB or the ministry before relying on them.

Videos. The video links came from web searches on the topic. I have not watched them and could not confirm which channel posted most of them, so do not read them as endorsements by the channels you named. The channel buttons open a YouTube search for that channel plus the topic. Highlighted buttons are my guess at the best fit. I was not sure which channel "IGR" is, so that button searches the letters IGR with the topic.
